Nasi Lemak


Description


Nasi lemak is a rice dish commonly served with roasted nuts, egg, ikan bilis (anchovies), and slices of cucumber. Literally meaning “fatty rice” in Malay, nasi lemak’s distinctive taste comes from cooking the rice in coconut milk and pandan leaves which gives the dish its rich flavour and fragrant aroma. It is rounded off with spicy sambal – a chilli paste made from dried chilies, garlic, shallots, and belacan (shrimp paste).


Ingredients

  • Coconut Rice : Rice cooked with rich coconut milk,salt and pandan leaves for flavor
  • Sambal : A hot sweet savory chili
  • Condiments : friend anchovies and roasted peanuts
  • Vegetables : Fresh, cooling slices of cucumber
  • Egg : Hard boiled egg

Steps For Cooking Nasi Lemak

  1. combine washed rice
  2. coconut milk
  3. water
  4. sliced ginger
  5. knotted pandan leaves
  6. and salt in a rice cooker and cook on the regular setting
